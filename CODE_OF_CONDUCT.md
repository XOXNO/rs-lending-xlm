# Code of conduct

## Purpose and scope

XOXNO Lending is developed in public by contributors, auditors, integrators,
maintainers, and community members. This code of conduct applies to repository
issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.

We want technical disagreement to be rigorous, direct, and respectful. The
protocol and its users benefit when people can raise concerns without
harassment, pressure, or public exposure of security-sensitive information.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, precise, and honest about uncertainty.
- Critique ideas, evidence, and technical decisions rather than people.
- Explain security concerns with concrete impact, assumptions, and alternatives.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project and welcoming to participants with
  different backgrounds and experience.
- Respect confidentiality and use the private disclosure process for suspected
  vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption, spam, or deliberately bad-faith technical discussion.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.
- Public disclosure of a suspected vulnerability before coordinated disclosure
  is complete.

## Reporting

For a code-of-conduct concern, email
[security@xoxno.com](mailto:security@xoxno.com) with any useful context, such
as links, screenshots, dates, and participants. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.

For a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. Do
not use public repository channels.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will assess reports based on severity, impact, context, and any
pattern of prior conduct. They may request clarification, remove content,
close or limit participation in a discussion, restrict project access, or
block an account.

Good-faith reports and technical disagreement do not lead to punishment.
Maintainers will communicate the outcome to the reporter when practical while
respecting the privacy of everyone involved.
